00012	Pluto Sextile Pluto Transit
00013	Pluto sextile Pluto transit occurs from your mid twenties for the older generation and into your forties for younger people (see table below).Â
00014	This transit brings changes in your life that are part of a creative evolution. You can see that you are moving toward your goals and don’t need anything else to attain them. You will get there simply by allowing the current energies to play themselves out.
00015	At the same time, you will be presented with additional opportunities for growth that you may or may not choose to take advantage of. This is a phase of stability within change.
00016	Your concerns will likely deepen as you become interested in the more profound aspects of your life. You may wish to understand more about internal psychological workings, and this is a good time to find out without fearing significant emotional distress.
00017	The overall gentleness of this transit allows you to discover many things that could be the source of anguish at other times. On another level, you will develop greater concern about the universe as a whole and where you are in relation to it.
00018	You may find that your views about daily life are stabilizing and maturing. Now that the period of rapid change is in the past, you are arriving at a way of dealing with the world that will not change much more, except, hopefully, when future experience invalidates your present views. In other words, this transit does not represent settling into rigidity, but stability.
