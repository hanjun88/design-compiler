00012	Pluto Opposite Pluto Transit
00013	Pluto opposite Pluto transit occurs in the 80s for those born in the 1950s, and progressively later for younger generations. (see table below). If you are in the older generations, this transit will occur in the same general time period as Uranus conjunct Uranus and Neptune opposite Neptune. The only similar clustering of transits from these planets occurs in mid-life in your thirties and early forties.
00014	This period represents the fulfillment of those changes that were made at that earlier time. You should examine the degree to which your life has been complete and rounded. Have you managed to achieve balance among the various things and issues that compete for your attention? The changes that occur during this transit are likely to be the result of whatever was not done thoroughly enough at that time.
00015	However, one seldom has lived life in a perfectly balanced manner, and no matter how hard you try, there will be some things that you have to deal with now that you did not feel previously. At this time, it may seem that these changes are unnecessary and intrusive. You may feel that you have lived a long life and should have some stability now.
00016	But change is always part of life. Allow such necessary changes to occur without obstruction, and most importantly, do not view them with regret. You are still changing and growing, and you will do so as long as you live. Always remember the old Buddhist saying, âEnlightenment is easy for those who have no preferences.â
