00014	You may be motivated by what you believe are good intentions. Still, Pluto’s extreme nature and the opposition’s antagonistic nature can lead to such behavior as obsessions, compulsions, jealousy, revenge, domination, and even megalomania and violence.
00015	If you exhibit any of those more negative manifestations of Pluto, you will face strong opposition in personal and professional relationships, intense power struggles and conflict. The very nature of Pluto means that you may swing from one extreme to the other in the process of finding that balance between your rights and those of others.
