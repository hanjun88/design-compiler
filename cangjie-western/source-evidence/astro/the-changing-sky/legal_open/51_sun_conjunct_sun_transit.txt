00012	Sun Conjunct Sun Transit
00013	Sun conjunct Sun transit is considered the most important of transits; it’s your birthday after all. Solar return charts are a crucial predictive tool in astrology, based on this date to provide insights into the year ahead. This transit is about a new start for the year, marking new beginnings.
00014	A time for your personal New Year’s resolutions. The Sun is life’s energy source so that you can expect an energy boost and increased vitality. Individuality and expression of that come more easily because you are in focus. The extra energy is best utilized by clearly expressing your desires and goals, and then taking action to move forward.
00015	This interpretation for a Sun conjunct Sun transit can also be applied to a Solar Eclipse and a New Moon conjunct Sun
