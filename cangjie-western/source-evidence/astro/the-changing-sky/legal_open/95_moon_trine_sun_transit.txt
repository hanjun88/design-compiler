00011	Moon Trine Sun Transit
00012	Moon trine Sun transit is one of the best Moon transits or Moon Phases you will have because it makes you feel totally at ease physically, mentally and emotionally. You will feel comfortable and confident being yourself without pretense or pretending.
00013	If you have to work, you can focus on the job with ambitious energy and do it well. If you want to relax, you will be able to without feeling that you should be doing something else. You should not encounter obstacles or major drama with anything you need to do.
00014	Your inner peace will make all your relationships more harmonious than usual. Relationships in your private, social and professional life should be pleasant and cause no stress. Your calming influence on others means this is a good time for socializing and teamwork. You can take the lead if you want or join in; it’s up to you. This is also a good time for promoting yourself and your goals in public.
