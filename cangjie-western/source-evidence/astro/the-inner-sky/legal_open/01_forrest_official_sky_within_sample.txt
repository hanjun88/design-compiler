00017	bad choices, and dead ends. It lists your resources. And it talks about how your life

00019	yourself in the foot, in other words.

00033	00034	## Your Ten Teachers
00035	00036	Freud divided the human mind into three compartments: ego, id, and superego. Astrologers
00037	do the same thing, except that our model of the mind differs from Freud's in two
00038	fundamental ways. First, it's a lot more elaborate. Instead of three compartments, we
00039	have ten: Sun, Moon, and the eight planets we see from Earth. As we'll discover, each
00040	planet represents more than a "circuit" in your psyche. It also serves as a kind of
00041	"Teacher," guiding you into certain consciousness-triggering kinds of experience.
00042	00043	The primary celestial teacher is the Sun. What does it teach? Selfhood. Vitality. How
00044	to keep the life-force strong in yourself. If the Sun grew dimmer, so would all the
00045	planets… if you fail to stoke the furnaces of your own inner Sun, then you'll simply
00046	be "out of gas." All your other planetary functions will suffer too.
00047	00048	When you were born the Sun was in Libra. Perfect equilibrium. That's the spirit of the
00049	Scales. When Libra realizes its evolutionary aim, the nervous system is as still as a
00050	dark pool on a windless summer evening. Outwardly, Libran energy often looks as though
00051	it's already there: it seems graceful and balanced, even unflappable. Inwardly, it's
00052	another story: the Libran part of you is tuned as tight as the high string on a violin.
00053	00054	Watch a ballet, or any other beautiful thing. The outer harmony will internalize; you'll
00055	sigh, releasing tension. That's the Libran evolutionary strategy in a nutshell: flood
00056	your senses with perceptions of beauty. It will soothe you, lifting you closer to the
00057	unbreakable serenity which is the true goal of this sign of the zodiac.
00058	00059	With your Sun in Libra, on the deepest level of your character you are an artist.
00060	You were born with an instinctive tolerance for paradox. Everything has two sides, and
00061	you'll almost always consider both. This gives great clarity of mind, but also presents
00062	a problem. Be careful you don't get caught between two equally attractive or
00063	(unattractive!) possibilities and just freeze there.
00064	00065	## Houses
00066	00067	When you were born, that solar light illuminated the Fifth house. What does that
00068	signify? Start by realizing that Houses represent twelve basic arenas of life. There's
00069	a House of Marriage, for example, and a House of Career. Always, we find an element of
00070	"fate" in our House structures; the "Hand of God" continually presents us with
00071	existential and moral questions connected with our emphasized Houses. How we react and
00072	what we learn — or fail to learn — is our own business.
00073	00074	Pleasure — that's Fifth House territory. It's as though God marched you off the end of
00075	the cosmic diving board with the words, "Go down there and try to have a good time!"
00076	00077	## The Moon
00078	00079	The next step in our journey through your birthchart carries us to the Moon. As you
00080	might expect, Luna resonates with the magical, emotional sides of your psyche. It
00081	represents your mood, averaged over a lifetime. As the heart's teacher, it tells you
00082	how to feel comfortable, how to meet your deepest needs. While the Sun lets you know
00083	what kinds of experiences and relationships help you feel sane, the Moon is concerned
00084	with another piece of the puzzle: feeling happy.
00085	00086	## The Primal Triad (Sun / Moon / Ascendant)
00087	00087	There's a third critical piece in your astrological puzzle — the Ascendant, or rising
00088	sign. Along with the Sun and Moon, it completes the "primal triad." What is it? What
00089	does it mean? Simple — the Ascendant is the sign that was coming up over the eastern
00090	horizon at the instant of your birth. It's where the sun is at dawn, in other words. In
00091	exactly the same way, the Ascendant represents how you "dawn" on people — that is, how
00092	you present yourself. It's your "style," or your "mask."
00093	00094	Astrologers use the primal triad of Sun, Moon, and Ascendant in much the same way
00095	people who know just a little astrology use Sun signs. The difference is that while
00096	there are only twelve Sun signs, there are 1728 different combinations of all three
00097	factors. So when we say that you are a Libra with the Moon in Libra and Gemini rising,
00098	that's a very specific statement.
00099	00100	## Archetype Translation
00101	00101	Traditionally, signs are connected with Bulls and Sea-Goats and Scorpions — creatures
00102	we don't see every day. But we can translate those images into more modern archetypes.
00103	We can say you are "The Artist", or "The Diplomat", or "The Lover". Those are just
00104	different ways of saying you have the Sun in Libra. We can add that you wear the mask of
00105	"The Storyteller", or "The Journalist", or "The Witness". Those images capture the
00106	spirit of your Ascendant, which is Gemini.
00107	00108	You can combine those archetypes any way you want. And you can go further: Once you
00109	have a feel for the three basic signs in your primal triad, you can make up your own
00110	images to go with them. Whatever words you choose, those simple statements are your
00111	fundamental astrological signature. It's your skeleton. Our next step is to begin adding
00112	flesh and hair to that skeleton by considering the planets.
00113	00114	## Uranus as Individuation
00115	00116	If Uranus were the only planet in the sky, we'd all be so independent we'd still be
00117	Neanderthals throwing rocks at each other. There would be no language, no culture, no
00118	law. On the other hand, if Uranus did not exist, we'd all still be hauling rocks for
00119	Pharaoh. All individuality would be suppressed. This is the planet of individuation…
00120	the process whereby we separate out who we are from what everybody else wants us to be.
00121	Always it indicates an area of our lives in which, to be true to ourselves, we must
00122	"break the rules" — that is, overcome the forces of socialization and peer pressure.
00123	In that part of our experience, what feeds our souls tends to annoy mom and dad… and
00124	all the "moms" and "dads" who lay down the law of the tribe.
00125	00126	## Mercury as Thinking
00127	00127	Mercury buzzes around the Sun in eighty-eight days, making it the fastest of the
00128	planets. It buzzes around your head in exactly the same way: frantically. It's the part
00129	of you that never rests — the endless firing of your synapses as your intelligence
00130	struggles to organize a picture of the world. Mercury represents thinking and speaking,
00131	learning and wondering. It is the great observer, always curious. It represents your
00132	senses themselves and all the raw, undigested data that pours through them.
00133	00134	## Neptune as Trance / Transcendence

00139	in a Sign for an average of a little over thirteen years, so its Sign position actually
00140	describes not only you, but your whole generation. Its House position, however, is more
00141	uniquely your own.
