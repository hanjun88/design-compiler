00005	One of the most challenging transitions when you are learning astrology is growing
00006	beyond sun signs.
00007	00008	The signs are like the alphabet of astrology, and learning how to put them together
00009	with planets, houses, and aspects when reading a birth chart is like growing from
00010	knowing your ABCs to being able to read a novel.
00011	00012	The Inner Sky by Steven Forrest is my favorite book for beginners. It was originally
00013	published in 1984, but it is a classic that has aged well.
00014	00015	## Astrology as a Road Map to Happiness
00016	00017	The book begins by exploring Steven Forrest's philosophy as an astrologer. Instead of
00018	using astrology as a tool to describe the personality or predict the future, Forrest
00019	sees it as a road map to happiness. Astrology is meant to help you understand how to
00020	make decisions that will lead to the most fulfilling life for you as an individual.
00021	00022	Forrest doesn't assume that people naturally identify with their signs. Instead, he
00023	teaches that the signs describe virtues we are being challenged to live up to. The
00024	signs are ways of being in the world that will feel most natural when people have the
00025	courage to express them.
00026	00027	Instead of saying that Aquarius is weird and tends to rebel against cultural norms,
00028	for example, Forrest presents having an Aquarius planet as a challenge. "Aquarius and
00029	conformity: they mix just about as well as peace and nuclear warheads," he says. "To
00030	conquer the tribal instinct, the Water-Bearer must cultivate an absolute loyalty to
00031	truth. She must say what she sees regardless of the consequences."
00032	00033	## How to Read a Birth Chart
00034	00035	When reading a birth chart, there are three symbols you need to synthesize: planets,
00036	signs, and houses. These symbols are like coordinates in a physical map. They describe
00037	the shape of your soul, which creates the story of your life.
00038	00039	The planets are ways of talking about parts of your psyche. Each person has, for
00040	example, the drive to form relationships, the drive to communicate, and the drive to
00041	set limits and boundaries. These drives are assigned to the planets from Mercury to
00042	Pluto, as well as the sun and moon.
00043	00044	Everyone has the same basic needs and drives represented by the planets, but we all
00045	prefer to get those needs met in different ways. These differences are described by the
00046	signs. Each of the signs describes a virtue we are trying to learn how to express
00047	through the parts of ourselves represented by the planets.
00048	00049	Aries, for example, represents the desire to learn courage. If a person has Venus (the
00050	drive to form relationships) in Aries, that person is learning how to be courageous in
00051	their relationships. They might need to have the courage to be friends with people who
00052	have different beliefs than them, or they might need to have the courage to assert
00053	themselves when someone crosses their boundaries.
00054	00055	The last symbols are houses. Houses describe the classrooms for the lessons of the
00056	signs. If the person with Venus in Aries has that planet in the House of Home, they are
00057	going to have the most opportunities to learn how to be brave at home. If they have
00058	Venus in the House of Long Journeys Over Water, their need to learn courage in
00059	relationships will drive them to seek opportunities to be brave in the company of
00060	strangers.
00061	00062	Once Forrest has given you all the pieces necessary to interpret a birth chart, he
00063	walks you through how to synthesize the information into a complete picture of the map
00064	of your soul using the example of a celebrity chart. It is as if he is allowing you to
00065	look over his shoulder while he does a consultation for a client.
00066	00067	Ultimately, that is the gift of Steven Forrest's astrology: It is a brutally honest
00068	warning about the potential pitfalls of the human journey blended with compassion for
00069	the inevitability of mistakes.
