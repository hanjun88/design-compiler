L0001  (Heaven's Child) In contrast to traditional prediction-oriented astrology, this
L0002  new approach is based upon the law of karma and the urge toward
L0003  self-transformation. It focuses on the understanding and use of astrology as a
L0004  tool for spiritual and psychological growth, especially emphasizing the
L0005  transformative and karmic significance of Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto,
L0006  and places all astrological techniques in a growth-oriented perspective.
L0007  (Biblio) Demonstrates the use of astrology - primarily through analysis of the
L0008  natal chart and its progressions - as a tool for spiritual and psychological
L0009  growth. Scholarly exposition of Buddhist philosophy on astrology and Karma
L0010  (Sanskrit: deed, work, action, intention).
L0011  (Tim Burness) Astrology, Karma and Transformation: Inner Dimensions of the Birth
L0012  Chart (1978) is a modern classic, something of an 'astrological bible' to many.
L0013  The author has said that Saturn and Pluto transits were involved when he wrote it.
L0014  (Zenastral bibliography) Arroyo offers a lively, deeply psychological astrology
L0015  influenced by Jung. He emphasises the inner dynamics of the elements and the
L0016  role of the planets as forces of evolution rather than external influences.
L0017  (Cafe Astrology note) The work explores elements, transits and cycles, planetary
L0018  aspects, karmic relationships, the Ascendant, Midheaven, and opens the mind to
L0019  deeper meanings of the different 'dimensions' of the natal chart.
