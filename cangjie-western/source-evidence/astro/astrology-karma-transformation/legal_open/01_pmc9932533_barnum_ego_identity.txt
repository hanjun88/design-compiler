L0001  One of the ancient personality assessments is astrology. Certain astrological
L0002  beliefs classify people into 12 astrological types based on an individual's date
L0003  of birth, believing that each type has a different personality. To this day,
L0004  Astrology is popular and influential worldwide. However, astrology is regarded
L0005  as unscientific by the academic community.
L0006  The Barnum effect, the tendency of people to accept general personality
L0007  descriptions as their own characteristics, provides a possible explanation for
L0008  how these low-validity personality assessments contribute to adolescent mental
L0009  health.
L0010  Marcia et al. operationally define ego identity into two dimensions: exploration
L0011  and commitment. The former refers to the period of exploring various possibilities
L0012  of personal identity and ideological beliefs. The latter describes making a firm
L0013  decision in such areas and investing in ideal activities. By combining these two
L0014  dimensions, ego identity can be classified into four statuses: (1) achievement
L0015  (strong commitment with full exploration), (2) foreclosure (strong commitment
L0016  without exploration), (3) moratorium (active exploration without commitment), and
L0017  (4) diffusion (the absence of both commitment and exploration).
L0018  Empirically, Lee and Park's (2014) experiment demonstrated that adolescents who
L0019  participated in astrology counseling developed higher self-awareness.
L0020  Clements (2020) pointed out that habitual engagement in astrology requires
L0021  individuals to make choices from the various identities and possibilities offered,
L0022  leaving them cognitively disorientated and therefore threatening to anxiety.
L0023  Although the MBTI test is judged invalid and unreliable, some dimensions of MBTI
L0024  are related to the Big Five dimensions, which indicates that the MBTI can interpret
L0025  individuals' personalities to some degree. Like astrology, the personality
L0026  information ranges from strengths and weaknesses, romantic relationships,
L0027  friendships to career paths.
L0028  The results support that the Barnum effect and ego identity together act as an
L0029  essential mediating path between MBTI/astrology-type use and mental health. The
L0030  Barnum effect then promotes adolescents' ego identity, ultimately increasing
L0031  subjective well-being levels and reducing anxiety and depression.
