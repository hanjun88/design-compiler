L0001  In modern psychological astrology, the nodal axis has been adopted as one of the
L0002  primary indicators of karmic direction and soul-level growth.
L0003  The South Node represents mastered skills, default tendencies, and patterns
L0004  brought from previous experience (whether understood literally as past lives or
L0005  psychologically as early conditioning and inherited traits). It is the comfort
L0006  zone: what feels natural and instinctive, the defenses and talents we fall back on
L0007  without effort.
L0008  The North Node represents the growth edge - the qualities, approaches, and life
L0009  areas that are unfamiliar but deeply necessary for this lifetime's development.
L0010  Because North Node energy is new and unfamiliar, it often feels scary, awkward, or
L0011  even repulsive at first. It requires conscious effort and courage.
L0012  South Node positivity: gifts the soul can draw from. Shadow: stagnation,
L0013  over-attachment, over-reliance on old patterns that limit growth.
L0014  The nodes are always exactly opposite each other; they form one evolutionary axis,
L0015  not two independent points.
