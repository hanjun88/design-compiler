L0001  The four elements - fire, earth, air, water - are the foundational temperament
L0002  categories of astrology, each governing three signs.
L0003  Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius): inspiration, courage, initiative, drive and
L0004  outward movement; passion and spirit.
L0005  Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn): practicality, patience, material intelligence,
L0006  endurance; builds and stabilizes; matter and form.
L0007  Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius): intellect, communication, social connection,
L0008  objectivity; thinks and connects; mind and relating.
L0009  Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces): emotion, intuition, feeling and depth; feels and
L0010  intuits; emotion and soul.
L0011  The element is astrology's quickest read on a chart: it reveals your dominant
L0012  mode of experiencing life and where your blind spots tend to be.
L0013  (Arroyo lineage, per open bibliography) The planets are read through the element
L0014  they occupy as inner qualities of energy, not as external events.
