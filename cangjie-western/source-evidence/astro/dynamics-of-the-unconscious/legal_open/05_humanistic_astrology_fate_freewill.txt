L0001  I remember reading in college that Scorpio is a heavily karmic sign, in Stephen
L0002  Arroyo's Astrology, Karma and Transformation, and being fascinated by the idea.
L0003  The term humanistic astrology wasn't a specific school headed by one individual,
L0004  but it represented a stream of astrological thought that seemed to originate from
L0005  Dane Rudhyar, which viewed the natal chart as a map for an individual's growth,
L0006  rather than a fixed set of fated events.
L0007  The natal chart is a snapshot of the sky at the moment of birth; humanistic
L0008  astrology reads it as a map of potentials and tensions, not a script. The planets
L0009  are read as psychological functions and drives, and aspects as internal dynamics
L0010  between those functions rather than as external events that will happen to the
L0011  person.
L0012  Karma in this tradition is not punishment or reward from a deity; it is the law
L0013  of cause and effect of consciousness - habitual ways of being that one keeps
L0014  repeating until they are made conscious and transformed.
