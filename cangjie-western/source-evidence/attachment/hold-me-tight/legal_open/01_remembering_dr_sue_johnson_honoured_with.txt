00011	Love in the 21st Century: Honouring a Legacy of Connection and Healing
00012	This past Canada Day weekend, as the nation recognized those who have shaped our society for the better, we were reminded of the incredible legacy of
00013	Dr. Sue Johnson
00014	, founder of the
00015	International Centre for Excellence in Emotionally Focused Therapy (ICEEFT)
00016	and a recipient of
00017	Canada’s highest civilian honour
00018	, the
00019	Order of Canada
00020	.
00021	In 2016, Dr. Johnson was appointed as a
00022	Member of the Order of Canada
00023	for her outstanding achievements, dedication to the community, and service to the nation. Her work revolutionized the way we understand love and emotional connection in relationships. As the developer of
00024	Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT)
00025	, she not only advanced the field of psychology but profoundly changed lives—couples, families, and individuals—across the globe.
00026	Dr. Johnson’s work combined scientific insight, therapeutic innovation, and heartfelt humanity. Her bestselling book,
00027	Hold Me Tight
00028	, brought EFT to the wider public, and her global training efforts continue to influence thousands of therapists today. Her leadership made ICEEFT a beacon for evidence-based care rooted in the science of bonding and attachment.
00029	Though we lost Sue just over a year ago, her voice and vision remain vibrant in our community. As we mark another Canada Day, we celebrate not only her national recognition but the personal transformations made possible by her work.
