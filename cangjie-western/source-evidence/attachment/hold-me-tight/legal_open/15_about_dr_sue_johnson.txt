00011	Dr. Sue Johnson — author, clinical psychologist, researcher, professor, popular presenter and speaker — was a leading innovator in the field of couple therapy and adult attachment. Sue was the primary developer of Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), which has demonstrated its effectiveness in over 35 years of peer-reviewed clinical research.
00012	Sue was also founding Director of the International Centre for Excellence in Emotionally Focused Therapy (ICEEFT) and Distinguished Research Professor at Alliant University in San Diego, California, and Professor Emeritus, Clinical Psychology, at the University of Ottawa, Canada.
00013	Dr. Johnson has received a variety of awards acknowledging her development of EFT and international training and certification program, and other significant contributions to the field of couple and family therapy and adult attachment, including the Order of Canada and the 2022 Lifetime Achievement Award from Psychotherapy Networker. (See list of awards below.)
00014	Written for the general public,
00015	Hold Me Tight, Seven Conversations for a Lifetime of Love
00016	(2008), is a self-help version of her ground-breaking research – how to enhance relationships, how to repair them and how to keep them. This best seller has sold over one million copies worldwide, been translated into 30 languages, and now has a companion,
00017	The Hold Me Tight Workbook
00018	, published in 2022. The book was developed into the Hold Me Tight® Relationship Education and Enhancement Program, with workshops taking place around the world and also offered in an online format (HoldMeTightOnline.com). The program has been translated into numerous languages and adapted for specific groups, such as for couples facing heart disease, for families with teens, for families with adult children, and for Christian couples.
00019	Sue’s second book for the general public,
00020	Love Sense, The Revolutionary New Science of Romantic Relationships
00021	(2013), outlines the new logical understanding of why and how we love – based on new scientific evidence and cutting-edge research. Explaining that romantic love is based on an attachment bond, Dr. Johnson shows how to develop our “love sense” – our ability to develop long-lasting relationships.
00022	Dr. Johnson’s best-known professional books include
00023	The Practice of Emotionally Focused Couple Therapy: Creating Connection
00024	(3rd edition, 2020),
00025	Emotionally Focused Family Therapy
00026	(2019), and
00027	Emotionally Focused Couple Therapy with Trauma Survivors
00028	(2002).
00029	Attachment Theory in Practice: EFT with Individuals, Couples and Families
00030	(2019), delineates the promise of attachment science in terms of making sense of and repairing our most precious relationships and thriving as strong resilient individuals. Attachment science offers us the most potent model for therapeutic change, especially when dealing with anxiety and depression, and that, of all models of intervention, EFT most closely captures the essence of the attachment perspective. EFT targets the defining feature of survival-oriented human connection, namely strong emotion, and systematically shapes core bonding interactions with others. Sue’s most recent professional book,
00031	A Primer for Emotionally Focused Individual Therapy
00032	(EFIT), published in 2021, highlights the unique benefits of EFT as a cross-modality approach for treating emotional disorders in individuals.
00033	Published in late 2022,
00034	Edgar and Elouise – Sagas 1 & 2
00035	, is Sue’s first work of fiction. It tells the story of a group of fascinating animals who learn to discover who they are, face fear and peril in the face of overwhelming odds and come together to fight to save their world.
00036	Dr. Johnson trained counsellors in EFT worldwide and consulted to the 89 international institutes and affiliated centers who practice EFT. She also consulted to Veterans Affairs Canada and the U.S. and Canadian militaries.
