00084	What is the difference between “certificate of completion” and “Certified EFCT/EFIT/EFFT Therapist”?
00085	A certificate of completion is offered by EFT event organizers to participants to indicate that they completed a course. A Certified Therapist is someone who has taken at least 40 hours of core EFT training and undergone at least 8 hours of supervision by a Certified Trainer, Certified Supervisor, or Supervisor Candidate.
00086	m
00087	What is the difference between online trainings through ICEEFT, Psychwire, and PESI?
00088	The Psychwire and PESI courses are a different platform that Sue Johnson and other trainers can use to share their knowledge and education. Only ICEEFT-approved courses will count towards your certification in EFT. The only exceptions are PESI EFIT 1 and 2 courses, which can count toward EFIT certification.
