# Evidence: Psychology Today — Internal Family Systems Therapy overview (8 Cs + 5 Ps)
# URL: https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/therapy-types/internal-family-systems-therapy
# License: Authoritative consumer health encyclopedia, open summary
# Lines numbered.

1  The Self has many positive traits, according to Schwartz's model, including the eight Cs and the five Ps.
2  The eight Cs are: Confidence, Calmness, Creativity, Clarity, Curiosity, Courage, Compassion, Connectedness.
3  The five Ps are: Presence, Patience, Perspective, Persistence, Playfulness.
4  The presence of these traits can help identify how much of the Self is available at a given time and how much of the Self may still need to emerge.
5  IFS holds that extreme parts are not the true self but protective subpersonalities, and that people can access Self-energy to relate to parts with curiosity and compassion.
