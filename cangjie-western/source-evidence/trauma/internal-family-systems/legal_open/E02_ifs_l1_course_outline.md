1  Module 3 is dedicated to working with parts of the personality that become "exiled" and how this phenomenon occurs.
2  Ways to identify "exiles" by their common traits and behaviors is examined with an emphasis on building a trusting relationship to Self.
3  IFS techniques to negotiate with exiles and prevent affect overwhelm are practiced to increase trust therapeutically and within the client's system.
4  The 8 primary steps to unburdening exiles are examined extensively.
5  A focus is placed on helping clients access Self-energy as the healing agent.
6  The model assumes that no part is inherently bad; all protective parts are seen as performing a protective function that was once necessary.
7  Working with exiles must proceed only with the permission of protective parts; forcing access risks affect overwhelm and retraumatization.
