1  Our oldest pathway of response, the dorsal vagal pathway of the parasympathetic branch, is the path of last resort.
2  When all else fails, when we are trapped and action taking doesn't work, the "primitive vagus" takes us into shutdown, collapse, and dissociation.
3  Here at the very bottom of the autonomic ladder, I am alone with my despair and escape into not knowing, not feeling, almost a sense of not being.
4  I might describe myself as hopeless, helpless, worthless, dead, or gone.
5  The middle rung, the sympathetic branch, is the pathway of mobilization: fight or flight, agitation, anxiety, rage, and high energy directed outward or inward.
6  The top rung, the newest vagal pathway (ventral vagal), is the pathway of safety and social engagement: connection, calm, playfulness, and the capacity to listen and be listened to.
7  Regulation is not a destination but a rhythm: the system moves up and down the ladder as neuroception detects shifts in safety, danger, or life-threat.
