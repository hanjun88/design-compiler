00064	Suggested Intentions for Recovery
00065	Normal and safe wants and needs to wish and hope for...
00066	to cultivate with mental, spiritual and emotional energy.
00067	I want to develop a more constantly loving and accepting relationship with  myself.  I want an increasing capacity for self acceptance.
00068	I want to learn to become the best possible friend to myself.
00069	I want to attract, into my life, relationships that are based on love, respect, fairness and mutual support.
00070	I want to uncover a full, uninhibited self expression.
00071	I want to attain the best possible physical health.
00072	I want to cultivate a balance of vitality and peace.
00073	I want to attract, to myself, loving friends and loving community.
00074	I want increasing freedom from toxic shame.
00075	I want increasing freedom from unnecessary fear.
00076	I want rewarding and fulfilling work.
00077	I want a fair amount of peace of mind, spirit, soul and body.
00078	I want to increase my capacity to play and have fun.
00079	I want to make plenty of room for beauty and nature in my life.
00080	I want sufficient physical and monetary resources.
00081	I want a fair amount of help (self, human, or divide) to get what I need.
00082	I want God's love, grace and blessing.
00083	I want a balance of work, rest and play.
00084	I want a balance of stability and change.
00085	I want a balance of loving interaction and healthy self sufficiency.
00086	I want a full emotional expression with a balance of laughter and tears.
00087	I want sexual satisfaction.
00088	I want to find effective and non-abusive ways to deal with anger.
00089	I want all this for each and every other being.
00090	(excerpted from The Tao of Fully Feeling, by Pete Walker, MFT)
