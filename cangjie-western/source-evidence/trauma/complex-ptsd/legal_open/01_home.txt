00067	Pete Walker
00068	is a licensed psychotherapist, MFC 25210, with degrees in Social Work and Counseling Psychology. He is a "general practitioner" who has a private practice in Berkeley, California, in the serene Claremont Hotel neighborhood. He has been working as a counselor, lecturer, writer and group leader for thirty-five years, and as a supervisor and consultant of other therapists for 20 years.
00069	Pete specializes in helping adults who were traumatized in childhood, especially those whose repeated exposure to abuse and/or neglect left them with
00070	the symptoms of Complex Post Traumatic Stress Disorder [Cptsd].
00071	I am pleased to tell you about my latest book,
00072	Holistically Treating Complex PTSD
00073	. It is textbook-like explication of the latest evolution of my work on treating Complex PTSD.  It is a guide for psychotherapists and other helpers to learn my multidimensional approach to effectively treating the childhood trauma that is commonly at the roots of Complex PTSD.
00074	My highly acclaimed approach is developed and advanced in this book in much greater detail. It incorporates, reorganizes and greatly expands the material of my earlier books. It pragmatically and accessibly explains how to treat the six key dimensions of damaged and arrested development suffered by most untreated survivors. These dimensions are the Relational, Behavioral, Cognitive, Emotional, Somatic, and Spiritual layers of their stultified innate Intelligence.
00075	This book can also serve as a teaching curriculum for educating training therapists to become highly effective trauma-informed therapists.

00122	In Part I of
00123	Homesteading in the Calm Eye of the Storm
00124	Pete escapes from his dysfunctional family and backpacks around the world seeking happiness, while unconsciously fleeing his suffering. In Part II, he wanders the jungles of psychological theory and technique, and shifts his focus from global adventurer to inner world explorer. Gradually, over time his fear shrinks, his toxic shame melts away, and peace of mind becomes his touchstone. His psyche gradually heals as his capacity for self-kindness, self-care, and self-protection continuously grow.
