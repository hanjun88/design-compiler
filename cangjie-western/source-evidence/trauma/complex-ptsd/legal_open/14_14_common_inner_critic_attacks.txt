00004	neglectful family. I do not have to be perfect to be safe or loved in 
00005	the present. I have a right to make mistakes. Mistakes do not make 
00006	me a mistake.  Every mistake is an opportunity to practice loving

00008	2. All-or-none  / black-and-white  THInking

00010	criticisms. One negative event does not mean I am stuck in a never-
00011	ending pattern of defeat. Statements that describe me in “always” or 
00012	“never” are rarely true. 
00013	3. self-hate / Self-disgust  / toxic shame
00014	I commit to loving myself.  I am a good enough person.  I refuse to 
00015	trash myself.  I transfer back the shame and disgust to the person 
00016	who is judging me for my normal emotions of anger, fear and sadness. 
00017	I especially refuse to attack myself for how hard it is to completely
00018	eliminate the self-hate habit.

00082	10. drasticizing / catastrophizing  / hypochondriasizing
00083	I feel afraid but I am not in danger. I am not in trouble with my 
00084	parents anymore. I refuse to scare myself with thoughts and 
00085	pictures of my life deteriorating. I will not turn every ache into a 
00086	story of me dying.  I am safe and at peace. 
00087	11. Negative focus
00088	I renounce over-noticing and dwelling on what might be wrong with me 
00089	or life around me. I will not minimize or discount my attributes. Right 
00090	now, I notice, visualize and enumerate my accomplishments, talents 
00091	and qualities.  I remember the many gifts and beauties of life. 
00092	12. Time urgency
00093	I am not in danger. I do not need to hurry. I am learning to enjoy living 
00094	at a relaxed pace. I slow down my breathing and relax my muscles.
