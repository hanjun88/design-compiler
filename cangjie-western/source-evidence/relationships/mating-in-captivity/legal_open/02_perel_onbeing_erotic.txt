L00005	## Eroticism vs sexuality
L00006	My book and my work is about eroticism. It is about how people connect to this quality of aliveness, of vibrancy, of vitality, of renewal. And that is way beyond the description of sexuality. It is a transcendent experience of life, because it is an act of the imagination.
L00007	
L00008	Animals have sex - it is the instinct, the base. But we have an erotic mind. And that erotic mind is infinite. Eroticism thrives on the ritual and the celebration and the infiniteness of our imagination - and on the forbidden, for that matter, too.

L00012	## Erotic as antidote to death
L00013	The people who came back to life had less survivor guilt. They understood the erotic as an antidote to death: how do you keep yourself alive in the face of adversity?
L00014	
L00015	You can't be on guard and let go. And playfulness comes with a certain element of letting go. L00036	## Safety + play = erotic
L00037	Play and curiosity are so intimately interwoven. Those are probably two of the most central elements - imagination, playfulness, curiosity - which go with risk. Risk is when... play is when risk is fun. But you can't play when you are in a situation of danger, anxiety, or contraction.
L00038	
L00039	So you have to feel safe in order to play. But if you do not play, you won't experience the erotic.

L00041	## Novelty is not positions
L00042	Novelty is not about new sexual positions. Novelty is new experiences of yourself in the world and of your partner in relationship to you. It involves taking risks by having an active engagement with the unknown.

L00044	## Me and us tension
L00045	We all come into this world with a need for connection and protection and with a need for freedom. And from the first moment on, we will be straddling these two needs: what is me, and what is us?
L00046	
L00047	You discover who you are, in the presence of another. This constant dance between me and you, between I and thou, is at the core of being human.
L00048	
L00049	- What right do I have to do for me when it hurts you?
L00050	- How much can I ask for me and not give to you?
L00051	- How much do I give to you until I feel that I have not given enough to myself?
L00052	- How much do I make sure not to lose you but lose me in the process?
L00053	- Or how much do I have to hold onto me but lose you in the process?
L00054	
L00055	That tension, that dance, for me, is very much at the core of being human - freedom and responsibility.

L00057	## Reinvent on location
L00058	Those people who do it with the same person - that is erotic intelligence, because they're able to reinvent themselves on location and to create a newer relational arrangement with each other.
